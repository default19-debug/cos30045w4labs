Exercise 4.1 — Customised SVG house and garden

The initial drawing, rebuilt and extended by hand. Turn on the coordinates to see where a sample of the shapes sit in the SVG's coordinate system: the origin (0, 0) is the top-left corner, x grows to the right and y grows downwards. The drawing uses a viewBox of 0 0 1000 750, so every number below is in those units, whatever size the page is.

What changed from the initial drawing

  • Roof rebuilt as a path with a trim line, plus a chimney with a curling smoke trail.
  • Windows redrawn with frames, crossbars and sills, defined once and placed twice with <use>.
  • Added a round attic window, door panels, a house number and a doorstep.
  • The two orange lines became a filled, winding garden path with stepping stones.
  • New garden features: fruit tree, mailbox, flower beds and a picket fence.
  • Sky detail: a sun with rotated rays, reusable clouds, birds, rolling hills and gradient sky and lawn.
  • Colours moved from inline fill attributes into CSS classes.

SVG features used

RequirementWhere it appears
Shapesrect (wall, door, chimney, trunk), circle (sun, canopy, apples, attic window), ellipse (clouds, petals, stepping stones), line (rays, window crossbars, flower stems)
PathsRoof (M L Z), hills, smoke and garden path (cubic C), birds (relative quadratic q), mailbox (arc A), pickets
Text"Home sweet home" label and the house number on the door
Grouping & transforms<g> for the sun, tree and building; translate on the sun, tree, windows, clouds, birds, flowers and pickets; scale on the second cloud, a bird and some flowers; rotate on the sun's rays and flower petals
StylingCSS classes in a <style> block, linear gradients, opacity, rounded line caps and joins, currentColor to recolour reused flowers
