Television models registered per brand, Australia 2026

Brands with at least 20 models that are available and sold in Australia. Samsung, Kogan and LG alone account for 60% of the 4,240 models — more than the other 22 brands combined. Kogan, an Australian brand, is highlighted.

Source: Energy Rating data for household appliances — televisions, data.gov.au (tv_2026_02_15.csv), processed in KNIME.

How the scales work

  • Linear scale for count (continuous): maps 0 → the largest count onto 0 → 900 SVG units, so the longest bar always fits.
  • Band scale for brand (categorical): splits 740 units of height into one equal band per brand.
  • Padding of 0.2 leaves a 20% gap between bands, so the bars stay visibly separate.
